# Quality Checklist

Use this before final delivery.

## Boundary

- Is the industry boundary explicit?
- Did the analysis avoid drifting into a single-company profile?
- Are adjacent industries and substitutes named?

## Chain

- Is the chain shown from upstream to downstream?
- Are bottleneck nodes distinguished from ordinary nodes?
- Are true beneficiaries separated from concept exposure?

## Data

- Are the last two years of data reviewed where available?
- Are data sources ranked by reliability?
- Are missing data points marked as missing?
- Are AI-generated statements verified before becoming facts?
- Does each major section meet its minimum evidence count or explicitly mark an evidence gap?
- Are source date, geography, and data definition visible for important numbers?

## Supply-Demand

- Is demand source clear?
- Is supply constraint clear?
- Are capacity, yield, expansion plan, and ramp-up time separated?
- Is announced capacity separated from effective capacity?

## Cycle

- Is the current cycle stage named?
- Are signals for the next stage listed?
- Is there at least one disconfirming signal?

## Capital Market

- Is industrial reality separated from market expectation?
- Does the report avoid direct buy/sell recommendations?
- Does it explain whether the market may already have priced the shortage or growth?

## Reasoning

- Are facts, inferences, and assumptions separated?
- Are the biggest uncertainties visible?
- Does the report explain what to track next month?

## DeepSearch Controls

- Was each subtask capped at a fixed number of search rounds?
- Were repeated sources stopped instead of browsed indefinitely?
- Were full logs, journals, JSONL files, and raw traces avoided?
- Were long observations compressed before entering final reasoning?
- Are unresolved conflicts preserved instead of averaged away?

## Profit Transmission Chain

- Is the profit/order transmission chain mapped from end demand to upstream?
- Are profit pool shifts identified at each stage of the cycle?
- Is the timing lag between demand signal and upstream order quantified?
- Are pricing power distributions clear (who captures, who absorbs)?

## Cycle Timeline

- Are historical cycle turns documented (if data available)?
- Is the current position on the timeline estimated with date anchors?
- Is the expected transition to the next phase identified?
- Is there a visual timeline showing where the industry currently sits?

## Observation Posts

- Are 3-5 specific watchpoints defined with measurable indicators?
- Does each watchpoint have a data source, frequency, and trigger threshold?
- Is the signal meaning clear for each threshold breach?
- Is there an update cadence defined (monthly review, quarterly reassessment)?

## Database-Style Tracking

- Is there a structured "Latest Update" section with fields, values, dates, and sources?
- Is there a "History Log" for tracking events and their cycle stage impacts?
- Are tracking fields aligned with the observation posts?

## Delivery Verification

### Static Conclusion Check

- Does the report contain at least one forward-looking conditional statement?
- Are there specific trigger thresholds, not just "monitor" or "track"?
- Does the report say what would change the conclusion, not just state the conclusion?
- Is there a "what would prove this wrong" section that is specific, not generic?

### Vague Risk Warning Check

- Does each risk have a specific trigger condition (not "market may decline")?
- Does each risk have an estimated probability or confidence level?
- Are risks tied to specific indicators from the observation posts?
- Is there a clear distinction between "risk exists" and "risk is likely"?

### Actionability Check

- Can a reader take specific action based on the report?
- Are recommendations conditional (if X happens, then Y)?
- Is the tracking plan specific enough to execute without re-reading the full report?
- Are the observation posts designed to be updated, not just listed once?
